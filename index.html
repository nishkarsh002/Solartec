About Us

NOVEX POWERTECH PRIVATE LIMITED specializes in industrial oil filtration, purification and transformer maintenance solutions. With 11+ years of industry experience, we offer transformer oil filtration plants, online dry-out systems, hydraulic and turbine oil purification machines, diesel and coolant filtration systems, BDV testing equipment and genuine spare parts. We also provide repair, refurbishment, installation, commissioning and maintenance services. ISO 9001:2015 certified, we are committed to quality, reliability, customer satisfaction and sustainable oil reuse. Powering Performance, Purifying the Future.
